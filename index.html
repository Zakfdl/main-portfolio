Strategic Approach

Operator First. Strategist Second.

I bridge technical store execution, behavioral analytics, and commercial revenue.

One day that means building a complete GA4 and GTM tracking container from scratch; the next, architecting custom Excel automation to eliminate ERP inventory sync errors; the next, drafting an evidence-based CRO test from Clarity heatmaps.

Working knowledge of HTML, CSS, and modern web architectures means I understand implementation, not just strategy — when I recommend a change to a product hierarchy or checkout flow, I know exactly what it takes to build it.

I specialise in the Saudi and GCC market, with deep hands-on expertise in Salla, alongside Shopify, Zid, WooCommerce, and Amazon Marketplace.

01
Funnel Diagnosis Before Ad Spend
Pages and checkout funnels are judged by their effect on conversion — Clarity heatmaps, GA4 drop-off funnels, and structured A/B tests replace subjective opinions before scaling ad spend.

Measurement Built from Ground Zero
You cannot optimize what you do not measure accurately. GA4 properties and GTM containers are built from scratch with custom e-commerce event triggers for solid attribution.
03
Technical SEO & GEO Readiness
Structuring product catalogs, clean taxonomy, and structured schema to capture high-intent commercial search while ensuring discoverability across emerging AI answer engines.
04
Operational Automation & Data Reliability
