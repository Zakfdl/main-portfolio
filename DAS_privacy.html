Privacy Policy for DAS Image

Effective date: October 24, 2023

DAS Image ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the DAS Image mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

DAS Image operates entirely offline. All image conversion, compression (JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF), resizing, and ZIP export processes occur locally on your device.

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.
  • We do not upload your photos or images to any cloud servers or external third parties.
  • All selected images and processing results remain in your device's local memory and are discarded when the app session ends or files are saved via system pickers.

2. Permissions

The application requires minimal system access:

  • Internet Access: None required. The app functions completely offline without an internet connection.
  • Storage / File Access: The app uses standard Android system document pickers (`ACTION_OPEN_DOCUMENT` and `ACTION_CREATE_DOCUMENT`) to let you select and save individual files. We do not have direct access to your file system outside of the files you explicitly choose to open or save.

3. Third-Party Services

DAS Image does not integrate analytics, tracking SDKs, or third-party advertising networks. Your usage is entirely private.

4. Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13, our app complies fully with COPPA and children's online privacy guidelines.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
